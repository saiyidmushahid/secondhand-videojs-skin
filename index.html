Into the canopy

The second hand

The big play button carries a clock ring. The hand sweeps the whole film, not the minute, so one glance tells you how far through the story you are. The ring fills in teal behind it.

A ruled timeline

The seek bar sits on a ruler: a tick every 10 seconds, a tall tick and a label every minute, amber breaks where chapters change. Scrubbing shows the exact time in big mono type before you let go.

Time, set in type

Every number is tabular mono, sized to read across the room. The current chapter is always named, top right and in the console, so you never scrub blind.

Keyboard first

Space or K play and pause   J / L back and forward 10s   ← → 5s   M mute   F fullscreen   0 to 9 jump to a tenth of the film. Visible focus rings everywhere, and motion calms down when your system asks for reduced motion.

Secondhand: an ejected Video.js v10 skin, rebuilt from the player's own components and restyled end to end. Film: Green by GawxArt, streamed by Mux (public playback ID shared in the challenge guidelines). Entry for the Video.js Skins Design Challenge on Contra.
